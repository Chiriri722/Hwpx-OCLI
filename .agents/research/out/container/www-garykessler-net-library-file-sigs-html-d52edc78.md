## GCK'S FILE SIGNATURES TABLE
#### 26 April 2025
  
  

This page will re-direct to the SEARCH site at [ https://filesig.search.org/](https://filesig.search.org/) in a few seconds...
  

> I started working on this page in February 2002 after finding a short list of file signatures (aka "magic numbers") in _Forensic Computing: A Practitioner's Guide_ by T. Sammes & B. Jenkinson (Springer, 2000). The site has been listed in Wikipedia's [ List of file signatures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_file_signatures) and several digital forensics books and course material documents in the last 20+ years. Although I am still active in a variety of ventures, I officially retired at the end of 2020 and I have veered away from forensics. Even though I kept making additions to the page, I asked the folks at SEARCH early in 2025 if they were interested in taking the page over and I am delighted that they have. Meanwhile, I remain available at  gck@garykessler.net.
> The last version of this page posted at my Web site can be found at [ file_sigs_GCK_latest.html](https://www.garykessler.net/library/file_sigs_GCK_latest.html).
> Thanks to everyone for your support over the years!!!
